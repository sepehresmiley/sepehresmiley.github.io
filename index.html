About

Sepehr Esmaeilipour is a Tehran-based systems strategist and software engineer who turns manual business operations into reliable software. He leads Intercodes, the software division of Interoffer Project-Management Corp., where he built Racketi, a sports facility booking platform.

His work connects systems strategy, product thinking and software engineering: understanding how a business runs, modelling its processes and data, and building practical tools people can use every day.

Based in Tehran, he works remotely with international clients and leads custom software and process-automation projects from requirements through delivery.
